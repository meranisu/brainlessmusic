## what's new (i touched a lot of stuff, pray for me)

- **it looks like the website now.** navy and orange, chunky title font for songs and albums, the logo got its own
  font. cards have a thin border instead of a blurry shadow because shadows are for SaaS dashboards and i am not one
- **colour themes.** settings → colours has all six from the website (deep blue, crimson, void, nord, magenta,
  pastel), plus **material you** which steals colours from your wallpaper (android 12+). they all do light and dark.
  i checked the contrast with a script so you can actually read stuff. mostly
- **settings got promoted to a tab.** bottom bar is library / search / settings now. **log out** lives at the bottom
  of settings, where log out buttons go to retire
- **pull down to refresh.** drag a list down, it reloads. library tabs, artist pages, album pages. the refresh
  buttons have been fired
- **filter your songs.** there's a "filter songs" box on the songs tab. type a bit of a title, artist or album. tap a
  song and it plays the matching ones from there, not your entire library like an animal
- **player is less cluttered.** the queue button is gone, tap the **up next** card instead, it even tells you how many
  songs are left. the play button also got some personal space
- **startup screen.** opening the app shows the icon while it wakes up instead of staring at you blankly

## upgrading

install over v0.2.0. you stay logged in. if something breaks, it was like that when i found it
