## What's new in this version

- **New look.** The app now matches the website: navy and orange, a bold title font for song and album names, and
  the brainlessmusic logo in its own font. Cards have a thin border instead of a shadow.
- **Colour themes.** Settings → Colours has the website's six themes (Deep blue, Crimson, Void, Nord, Magenta, Pastel),
  plus **Material You**, which takes its colours from your wallpaper (Android 12 and newer). Each one works in light
  and dark mode.
- **Settings has its own tab.** The bottom bar is now Library, Search and Settings. **Log out** is at the bottom of
  Settings.
- **Pull down to refresh.** Drag a list down to reload it, on the Library tabs and on artist and album pages. The
  refresh buttons are gone.
- **Filter your songs.** The Songs tab has a "Filter songs" box. Type part of a title, artist or album name. Tapping a
  song plays the matching songs from there.
- **Simpler player.** Tap the **Up next** card to see the queue. It also shows how many songs are left. There is
  more space under the play button.
- **Startup screen.** Opening the app shows the app icon while it gets ready, instead of a blank screen.

## Upgrading

Install over v0.2.0. You stay logged in.
