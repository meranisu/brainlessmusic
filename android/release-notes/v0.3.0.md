## what changed

this one's a big one, mostly how the app looks

- it looks like the website now: navy and orange, the same chunky font for song and album titles, and the logo
  font on the login screen
- settings → colours has all six themes from the website (deep blue, crimson, void, nord, magenta, pastel), plus
  material you, which takes its colours from your wallpaper on android 12 and up. they all have light and dark
- settings is a tab at the bottom now, next to library and search. log out moved to the bottom of settings
- pull a list down to refresh it. works on the library tabs, artist pages and album pages, so the refresh buttons are gone
- there's a box on the songs tab to filter by title, artist or album. tapping a song plays the matching songs from
  there, not your whole library
- the queue button on the now playing screen is gone. tap the up next card instead, it shows how many songs are left
- the app shows its icon while it starts up instead of a blank screen

## upgrading

install over v0.2.0, you stay logged in
