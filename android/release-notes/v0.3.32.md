## what changed

player skins are here, starting with osu!

- settings → appearance → **player skin** (it used to say player buttons). standard and pills are still there
- new skin: **hit circle**. round cover, buttons as numbered hit circles, a slider to seek, and an approach ring that keeps closing in on play while the music's going
- it uses whatever colour theme you have on. magenta is the osu!-ish one
- you also get your record for each song: plays, time listened, a grade and a lamp. finish a song 3+ times without skipping it and it's a full combo. skip it more than you finish it and... failed
- more skins coming, one game at a time
