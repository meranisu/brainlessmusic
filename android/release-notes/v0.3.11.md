## what changed

guests get a proper way in now:

- the Guest button opens a small form instead of letting you straight in. you can type a name, or leave it blank and
  be Guest plus a number
- got a guest code? tap "I have a code" and type it. the same code now signs you in as the same guest on any phone or
  browser, so your spot in the queue follows you around
- no code? tap "Request a code". the admin gets asked, and once they say yes the phone signs itself in and shows you
  your code. write it down or copy it, that's how you get back in on another device
- you can close the app while waiting, it picks the wait back up when you open it again
- Settings → Account shows your name and lets you change it

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. the guest stuff needs the server updated too, ask whoever
runs it
