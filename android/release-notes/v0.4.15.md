## what changed

- search got a proper redo. there's a top result up top with a play button, then songs first, then artists, albums, playlists and people, each with "see all"
- chips under the search box let you look at just songs, just artists and so on, and those lists keep loading as you scroll
- the words you typed are highlighted in the results
- albums in search show the year and have a play button, artists show how many songs they have
- before you type, you get your recent searches (tap one to search again, or the x to drop it) and genres to browse
- if nothing turns up you get a "did you mean" with the closest name, plus a few tips, instead of a blank page
- accents don't matter anymore when searching people and playlists either, so "zoe" finds "Zoë"
