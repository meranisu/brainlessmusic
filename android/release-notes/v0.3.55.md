## what changed

- new skin: ring, after maimai. the cover is the round screen, as wide as your phone, with the song's progress as the ring around it and eight buttons sitting on that ring like the cabinet
- the ring fills in a difficulty colour from how often you've played the song: basic, advanced, expert, master, re:master. the tab at the top says which, with your play count as the level
- drag around the ring to seek, a little star rides the end of it
- under the title you get an achievement % out of 101, a rank from D to SSS+, and CLEAR or FC
- home gets a rating plate, five digits that go from white up to rainbow
- new colour theme candy, pink, sky blue and yellow, and it actually looks right in light mode
- rounded font, M PLUS Rounded 1c, and new rounded icons
