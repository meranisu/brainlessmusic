## what changed

you can download music now, so it plays with no signal. this is the first go at it, so tell me if something's off

- albums and playlists have a download button next to play and shuffle. tap it again to remove the download
- single songs can be downloaded from the song menu (the ⋮ or long press), guests too
- new Downloads tab between Search and You, with everything on your phone. tap to play, shuffle icon to shuffle, ⋮ to retry or remove
- downloads are the original files, over wifi or mobile data, and they keep going if you close the app
- with no signal the app opens straight on Downloads, and a line at the bottom says you're offline
- if you're offline and the queue has songs that aren't downloaded, it skips them instead of stopping
- songs you play offline still count in your stats, they get sent once you're back online
- Settings has a Downloads page: how much space it's using, an optional storage limit, and delete all
- data saver doesn't touch downloaded songs, they're already on the phone
- opening the app with no signal used to log you out. my bad. it doesn't anymore
- search keeps the old results on screen while the new ones load, instead of going blank

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. downloads show a notification while they run. offline plays get the right time once the server is updated too, until then they're dated to when they were sent
