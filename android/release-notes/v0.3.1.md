## heads up

this version crashes when you open the library. skip it and get v0.3.2

## what changed

- the keyboard doesn't push the login screen off anymore, so you can actually reach the log in button
- the filter box under the tabs is gone. there's a search button next to the tabs instead. tap it to search songs,
  tap the X or go back to get the tabs back. searching from albums or artists takes you to songs, since that's all it
  searches
- songs, albums and artists have a scroll bar next to the letters now. drag it to get through a big library fast
- shuffle all is just the shuffle icon now
- song titles don't show through behind the tabs when you scroll anymore

## upgrading

install over v0.3.0, you stay logged in
