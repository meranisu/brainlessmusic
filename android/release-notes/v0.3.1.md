## what's new (small one, i swear)

- **the keyboard stopped bullying the login screen.** opening it used to shove the whole card up and hide the log in
  button. now the card just makes room like a normal app
- **search is a button now.** the filter box that sat under the tabs all the time is gone. there's a round search
  button next to the tabs. tap it, the tabs turn into a search box, the X next to it brings the tabs back. back
  button works too. searching from albums or artists takes you to songs, because that's the only thing it searches
- **scroll bars.** songs, albums and artists have one now, right next to the A–Z letters. grab it and drag to fly
  through the whole library. while you're searching the letters hide and the scroll bar takes over
- **shuffle is just an icon.** round button, shuffle arrows, same job. it didn't need a caption
- **song titles stopped peeking out** from behind the tabs when you scroll. they fade out underneath now

## upgrading

install over v0.3.0. you stay logged in. if the scroll bar and the letters fight over your thumb, tell me
