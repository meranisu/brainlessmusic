## what changed

- the mini player shows up on Home again after you fully close and reopen the app, no more going to Library first to get it back
- music should stop cutting out a couple of minutes after you leave the app. it was letting the phone put wifi and the cpu to sleep mid song
- equalizer support: equalizer apps like Wavelet or Poweramp Equalizer can now work with the app, and Settings > Playback has an Equalizer button that opens your phone's (or your eq app's) equalizer
