## what changed

skipping songs counts for something now

- before, a song only got saved once you'd heard half of it (or 4 minutes). skip around for half an hour and your home page still said 0
- now every song you actually hear gets saved. the ones you skip early show up in recently played and add to your minutes listened
- skips don't count as plays and stay out of your top songs, so the stuff you skip won't climb your charts
- offline skips wait on the phone and go up once you're back online, same as plays

also: turning off "keep playing when swiped away" should actually stop the music now when you swipe the app away. the notification might hang around paused, that's normal

## upgrading

the update sheet will offer it. the skip saving needs the server update too, which goes up at the same time
