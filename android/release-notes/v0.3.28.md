## what changed

listen together is on the phone now. everyone in a room hears the same song at the same moment, wherever they are

- tap the people icon at the top of home or now playing
- join with the six-letter code someone sends you, or start a room from whatever you're playing
- anyone in the room can pause, skip, seek and change the queue, guests too. play on a song adds it after the current one and jumps there for everyone
- the lock screen and headphone buttons control the room too
- share the code from the room screen. the host can remove people or end the room
- shuffle and repeat are off while you're in a room and come back when you leave. leaving doesn't stop the music, it carries on from where the room was
- a phone call or pulling your headphones out only pauses your phone, the room keeps going. press play to jump back in
- if your signal drops, the music keeps playing and it catches up when you're back

it also works with the website, so a phone and a browser can be in the same room. first version, so tell me if it drifts or does anything weird

## upgrading

install over v0.3.27, nothing else needed. the server already has the room stuff
