## what changed

your colour theme follows you now. pick crimson on the website and the app picks it up next time you open it, and
the other way round. material you is still a phone-only thing, so choosing it won't mess with what the website shows

a big pile of fixes, mostly playback:

- skipping, pausing and seeking from the lock screen, a headset or your car no longer gets the fades out of sync
- pressing next twice really skips two songs
- a phone call or pulling your headphones out no longer leaves the end of a song silent
- albums with two discs stay gapless across the disc change
- albums shuffle starts with the rest of the album you tapped
- likes and playlist edits save in the order you made them, and undo in a playlist keeps a drag you did in between
- failed refreshes and playlist changes now tell you instead of silently doing nothing
- the you tab shows the right numbers for the range you picked, and refreshes when you come back to it
- the splash screen matches the app's light or dark setting

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. theme syncing and a couple of the fixes need the server
updated too
