## what changed

- sleep timer: tap the moon at the top of Now playing and pick 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, or "end of current song". when it runs out the music fades out over about 10 seconds and pauses, and your queue stays so you can carry on tomorrow
- in a Listen together room the timer takes only your phone out of the room, everyone else keeps listening
