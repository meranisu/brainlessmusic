## what changed

- Listen together got a bunch of stuff to keep it fair when there's a few of you
- the host has room settings now: shuffle, repeat (queue or one song), take turns (one song from each person in rotation), and who's allowed to skip, play/pause, add songs, move or remove other people's songs, and invite
- skipping is a vote by default. if you added the song (or you're the host) Next skips straight away. anyone else's Next is a vote, and more than half the room skips it. with just two of you, one vote does it
- you see who voted as a little message, and the room card shows "1 of 2 votes to skip"
- every song in the room queue shows the picture of whoever added it
- Invite people button in the room: pick accounts and they get a notification. tapping it opens the room's join screen
- Friends: if someone's in a room, their row has a Join button

## heads up

invites only come through while the app is open or music is playing in the background. a fully closed app gets them next time it opens

## upgrading

the update sheet will offer it. the server needs the new version too (it updates itself after the push)
