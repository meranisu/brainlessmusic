## what changed

- hit circle and lane get their own listening card on home now, same idea as gear's radar. hit circle shows your listening as numbered hit circles that fill up, lane gives you an arcaea-style potential number in the diamond badge with bars under it
- hit circle's bottom bar: the open tab is a lit hit circle and an approach ring closes on it when you switch
- lane's bottom bar: the open tab is a lit slanted key instead of the diamond outline
- tab names in both use the skin's own font and stay on one line
