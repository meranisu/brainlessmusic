## what changed

hit circle isn't just the player anymore, it's the whole app

- settings → appearance → **skin**. pick hit circle and everything goes osu!: round covers, round avatars, softer corners, pill-shaped rows in the queue
- tap a song, an album, a card: a little approach ring closes in on your finger
- headings and buttons switch to the round logo font
- the mini player gets a slider line with a ball, and the tab you're on gets ringed
- the player is the hit circle one from last time
- **standard** is the app exactly as it was, and it's still the default
- colours are still their own thing, so try hit circle with magenta

## upgrading

the update sheet will offer it. if you picked hit circle last version, you're already wearing it everywhere
