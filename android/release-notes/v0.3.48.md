## what changed

- new skin: **gear**, after sound voltex. settings → skin
  - the player is a cabinet now: the lane with the red critical line climbing it as the song plays (drag it to seek), knobs for shuffle and repeat, white keys for back / play / next, orange keys for like and add
  - every song gets a score out of 10,000,000 from how often you finish it vs skip it, plus a grade (S to D) and a clear medal like ULTIMATE CHAIN
  - the gauge next to the lane turns pink once the song counts as a play
  - library tabs are the difficulty boxes, the queue numbers your tracks 01, 02, 03, and a lil intro when you switch to it
- new colour theme: **voltage**, navy and cyan to go with it
- new fonts and icons for gear: orbitron, chakra petch, phosphor (credited in licenses)
