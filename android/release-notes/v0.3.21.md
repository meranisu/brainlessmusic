## what changed

a bigger one this time, mostly the downloads and you tabs plus a pile of fixes from going over the whole app:

- the downloads tab is a grid of covers now. tap an album or playlist to see each song and download or remove them one by one
- downloads has play all and shuffle, a pause/resume all for what's still downloading, and filters for albums, playlists and songs
- the you tab got bigger: a player card, recently played, play and shuffle on your top songs, and arrows showing which songs and artists moved up or down since last time
- long press a song on the you tab for the song menu. the song details now show your own plays, time listened, and when you first and last played it
- settings got a new look, no more boxes everywhere. the cards in dark mode are easier to see now too
- if your login expires the app takes you back to the sign-in screen instead of quietly failing
- a song played twice by accident (resent after a bad connection) only counts once
- songs played offline get sent properly once you're back online, and downloads paused by a logout pick up again after you sign in
- the storage limit counts songs still waiting to download, so it doesn't go over
- if songs keep failing to play, it stops skipping after 5 in a row instead of looping forever on repeat
- opening the app from the notification player no longer leaves black bars at the top and bottom
- search doesn't flicker while you type anymore

## upgrading

install over v0.3.20, you stay logged in and your downloads stay.
