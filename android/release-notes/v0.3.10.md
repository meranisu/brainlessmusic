## what changed

new **You** tab in the bottom bar:

- your listening at a glance: hours listened, plays, how many songs. switch between this week, month, year or all
  time
- your top 10 songs and top artists, with how many times and how long you played each. tap a song to play your top
  list, tap an artist to open them
- liked songs, your playlists, and the songs you swiped to hear less, all one tap away

likes and playlists work on the phone now:

- heart button on the now playing screen, and like / unlike in an album's song menu
- add to playlist from now playing or an album's song menu, or make a new playlist right there
- playlists: play, shuffle, rename, delete, drag songs to reorder, swipe left to remove (with undo)

guests see a short note on the You tab, since plays and likes are only kept for accounts.

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. the You tab needs the server updated too, ask whoever
runs it
