## what changed

loading looks less like a frozen app now:

- instead of a spinner in the middle of the screen, you get grey outlines of what's coming: song rows, album
  covers, the album header. they gently pulse until the real stuff shows up
- on a fast connection you won't see them at all, they only show if loading takes a moment
- shows up on the library tabs (songs, albums, artists), album and artist pages, and search
- if you've turned animations off in your phone settings, they stay still

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in
