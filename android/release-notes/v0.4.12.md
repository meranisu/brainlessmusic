## what changed

- adding a song to a playlist got a proper sheet. it shows the song at the top, each playlist with its cover and song count, and a tick on the ones the song is already in
- tap a playlist to add the song, tap a ticked one to take it out. you can put it in a few at once, then hit Done
- got lots of playlists? there's a search box now, and if nothing matches you can make one with that name right there
- naming or renaming a playlist tells you what's wrong as you type: too long (100 characters max), or a name you already use (still allowed, just a heads up)
