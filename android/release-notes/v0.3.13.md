## what changed

two new kinds of shuffle. tap the shuffle button on the now playing screen and it goes off → shuffle → rediscover →
albums, the same way repeat cycles:

- rediscover (the clock icon) plays songs you haven't listened to in a while, or ever, sooner. everything still comes
  up eventually, the stuff you forgot about just jumps the line
- albums (the record icon) picks albums at random and plays each one start to finish, in order
- the queue shows which one you're on, and the shuffle-all button uses whichever you picked

rediscover works on the whole library. if you're playing an album or playlist it acts like normal shuffle, since the
phone doesn't know your listening history

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. the new modes need the server updated too, until then
they act like normal shuffle
