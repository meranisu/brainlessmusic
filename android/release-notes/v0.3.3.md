## what changed

- the status bar and nav bar don't go black in dark mode anymore. they did after the startup screen until you
  switched themes back and forth
- the A–Z strip on the right is the scroll bar now, instead of having both. drag it to scroll, or tap a letter to jump
- japanese, korean and other titles get their own spots on the strip after Z (あ, ア, 漢, 한 and so on), only for
  the ones your library actually has. before they were all lumped under one "…"
- tapping Library in the bottom bar when you're already there takes you back to the top
- the bar behind the tabs is solid now, so song titles don't show through it
- the logo is next to the Library title
- settings got tidied up: appearance, playback and account sections, the colour tiles show what each theme looks
  like, and it shows who you're logged in as and the app version

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in
