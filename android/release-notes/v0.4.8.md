## what changed

- playlists can be public now. open one of yours, tap ⋮ and pick make public, and everyone on the server can find it, play it and download it. make private takes it back
- the playlists tab has a from others section with everyone's public playlists, newest first, with who made them
- open someone else's playlist to play it or shuffle it, or tap ⋮ and save a copy to get your own version you can change. theirs stays as it is
- search finds playlists too: yours, and other people's public ones
- guests can see and play public playlists now (still no likes or playlists of their own)
