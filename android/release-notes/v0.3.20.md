## what changed

fixes for the downloads from the last update, found when going back over it:

- with data saver on, a song downloaded after it was queued could fail to play offline. fixed
- opening the app with no signal could briefly think nothing was downloaded. it waits for the list now
- hotel or cafe wifi that hasn't let you in yet counts as offline, so your downloads play instead of nothing
- repeat-all offline goes back round to the start instead of stopping
- songs played while the server was down (but your phone had signal) weren't saved for later. they are now
- offline plays from one account don't get counted for whoever logs in after. they're sent before logout, or dropped
- a song played offline could get counted twice, or with the wrong listening time. fixed

## upgrading

install over v0.3.19, you stay logged in and your downloads stay. the double-count fix needs the server update too
