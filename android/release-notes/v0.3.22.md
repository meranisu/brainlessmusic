## what changed

the you tab is now home, plus a bunch of smaller stuff:

- home is the first tab and where the app opens. it says hi, has a big shuffle my library button, then recently played, your library and your stats
- liked songs, playlists and songs you hear less are three tiles near the top now, no more scrolling to the bottom to find them
- switching week / month / year on home doesn't flash a blank screen anymore, the page stays up and fades while it loads
- long press a song and there's go to artist now, next to go to album
- playing something you downloaded? the player says "on this phone" instead of buffering and network speed. the data saver button hides when nothing in your queue would stream anyway
- the songs tile in downloads finally has covers, and the grid is three across so more fits
- songs in the downloads list show the album too
- settings is back to the old look, matching the rest of the app
- dark mode with the queue pulled all the way up no longer turns the status bar icons black
- the song details popup doesn't jump around while it loads
- shuffle tells you if it can't reach the server instead of doing nothing

## upgrading

install over v0.3.21, you stay logged in and your downloads stay. go to artist needs the server update too. songs you downloaded one at a time before this get their cover once you download them again
