## what changed

- found the real blank screen bug, thanks for spotting it. closing the player with a quick double tap (or tapping
  the arrow while it was still sliding away) closed the library behind it too, leaving nothing on screen. it can't
  do that anymore, same for the back arrows on albums and artists
- tapping the mini player twice doesn't open two players stacked on each other anymore
- new switch in settings → playback: "keep playing when swiped away". on by default, so music keeps going when you
  close the app from recent apps. turn it off if you'd rather swiping the app away stop the music

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in
