## what changed

- the playlists tab in the library got a proper glow up. liked songs is a big card up top with covers, play and shuffle, dressed up per skin (osu! collection, arcaea pack, sdvx favorite folder, maimai my list). your playlists show as a cover grid now instead of a boring list
- "hear less" is now called dislike. same thing as before, shuffle just picks the song way less. disliked songs live right under liked songs
- the heart button does both now: tap to like, hold to dislike, tap again to clear. liking pops the heart with a little burst in your skin's style, disliking cracks it in half
- a song can't be liked and disliked at the same time anymore
