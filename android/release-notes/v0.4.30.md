## what changed

- Now Playing: the room name moved off the top bar and sits as a little "Room ABCD" chip above the cover. tap it to open the room
- vote to skip actually feels like a vote now. the host's Next counts as one vote like everyone else's, and the songs a room started with belong to nobody, so they always need a vote. the song you added yourself still skips straight away. the Next button says "Vote to skip" when that's what it does, and you get a "Skipped by vote" note when a vote does it
- Listen together lobby: Start a room is one big button at the bottom
- room settings moved behind the gear in the top bar, so they're a tap away instead of at the very bottom
- the room's now playing card dropped the profile picture, and long titles scroll instead of getting cut off
- invites: the notification has Join and Reject buttons, and an invite banner shows above the mini player while the app is open. invites last 30 minutes now instead of 10
- heads up: a fully closed app still only hears about an invite once you open it. there's no push service yet
