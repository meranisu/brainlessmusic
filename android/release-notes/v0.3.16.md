## what changed

- hold a song (library, an album, a playlist, search results) and you get a menu: play next, like, add to playlist,
  go to album, play less often, and details like format, bitrate and play count
- holding the album cover on the now playing screen opens the same menu
- swipe the mini player left or right to skip, or up to open the player
- on the player, swipe the cover left or right to skip, and swipe down to close it
- the queue looks tidier: smaller text, lined up with the player, and the title and song count sit on one line
- data saver: streams smaller copies of songs, handy on mobile data. it's in Settings → Playback, or the icon at the
  top of the now playing screen. the song already playing finishes as it is, the next ones switch

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. no server update needed
