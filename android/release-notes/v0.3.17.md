## what changed

- albums and artists now have play and shuffle buttons at the top, same as playlists. since tapping a song turns
  shuffle off, this is the way to shuffle a whole album or everything by an artist
- shuffle buttons start on a random song now. a playlist's shuffle used to always open with its first song, my bad
- if you had rediscover or albums picked before v0.3.15, the first tapped song wiped that and shuffle came back as
  plain shuffle. it remembers your mode now

## upgrading

install over whatever version you have, you stay logged in. no server update needed
