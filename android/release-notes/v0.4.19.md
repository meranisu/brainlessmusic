## what changed

- songs you play now get saved on your phone (a cache), so playing them again uses no data and they work offline too
- offline? check the new "Cached" filter in Downloads, everything you've played fully is there. songs in your queue that are cached won't get skipped anymore when you lose signal
- pick how big the cache can get in Settings > Downloads: 500 MB, 1 GB (default), 2 GB, 5 GB or unlimited. when it's full the stuff you played longest ago goes first. there's a clear cache button too
- downloading a song you already played is instant now, it copies from the cache
