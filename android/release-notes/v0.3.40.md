## what changed

- new skin: **lane**, after arcaea. settings → appearance → skin
  - everything's in a thin wide font (exo 2), panels have their corners cut on the diagonal, rows are slanted, and the playing song gets a little diamond
  - new line icons to match
  - the player shows your song as a lane running into the distance. it fills up from the bottom as the song plays, and you drag up or down on it to seek. notes fall toward the line while it's playing
  - your record for the song shows arcaea style: track lost, track complete, full recall, or pure memory if you've played it 10+ times without skipping, with a grade up to EX+
  - tap a button and it says PURE
- new colours: **lavender**, lane's match. it lives on your phone only for now, the web doesn't have it yet
- switching skins now plays a little intro: hit circle lands a circle, lane slams the shutter shut and opens it. tap to skip it
