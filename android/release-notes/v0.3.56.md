## what changed

- volume levelling: songs now play at about the same loudness, so no more grabbing the volume when a loud track follows a quiet one
- it's on by default. turn it off in settings, under the fades
- the server has to measure every song first, which takes a while on a big library. songs you've played recently go first, and anything you play jumps the line, so it's levelled from the next time it comes up
- downloaded songs keep their levelling offline. songs downloaded before this update play as before until you download them again
- heads up: most of the library is mastered loud, so with levelling on things get quieter overall. turn your phone up a bit
