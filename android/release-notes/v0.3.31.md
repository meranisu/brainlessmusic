## what changed

home got a tidy up

- shuffle library and listen together are now two proper buttons right under your player card, instead of tiny icons up top
- your card at the top is smaller, so the good stuff shows up sooner
- top songs and top artists sit in their own cards, with play and shuffle at the bottom of the top songs card

new looks for the player, both in settings → appearance

- **player buttons → pills**: like, shuffle, repeat and add to playlist become a row of rounded buttons above the seek bar, and play turns into a hollow ring
- **progress bar → waveform**: the seek bar draws the actual song as bars, filling in as it plays. tap or drag to jump. offline it shows a stand-in shape
- mix them however you like. the old look is still the default

also the logo moved from the library title to the bottom of settings, next to the app name
