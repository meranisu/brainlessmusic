## what changed

- the server sorted out the albums. compilations and "feat." songs used to split one album into a pile of tiny ones (we had 14k albums for 32k songs), now it's about 3.4k proper albums
- on a compilation each song shows its own artist under the title, and the player and lock screen show it too
- albums with more than one disc get disc 1 / disc 2 headers and play in that order
- artist pages have an "appears on" section for albums where they're only featured, and play / shuffle on the artist includes those songs
- new: browse by genre. it's the "by genre" button at the top of the albums tab
- tap the artist under an album's title to open their page
- albums you downloaded before the shuffle-up still work, the app moves them to the merged album the next time you open it. downloaded compilation songs keep their own artist offline now
