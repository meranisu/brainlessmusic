## what changed

- profiles are here. tap your picture at the top left of home to see yours: your top songs, artists and genres for the week, month, year or all time, your playlists, and your liked and disliked songs
- tap edit profile to add a picture from your gallery, write a bio, and pin a favourite song and artist
- everyone else has one too. tap the person icon on home to see everyone, or search for someone by name
- names open profiles: tap "by someone" on a playlist, or someone's name in listen together
- other people only see your public playlists. your login name stays private, they just see your display name
- in a room, playing a song from someone's profile asks first, since it goes into the queue for everyone
