## what changed

- swiping the app away from recent apps now actually stops the music and gets rid of the notification, like spotify and yt music. before it only paused it, which wasn't the point. this is when "keep playing when swiped away" is off, or when the music is already paused
- your spot is still saved, so opening the app again picks up the last song
- closing the app that way doesn't count as skipping the song anymore
