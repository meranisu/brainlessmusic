## what changed

- playlists can have a description now, private ones too. add one from the playlist's menu (edit name and description) or tap "add a description" at the top. it shows on the playlists grid as well
- the playlist page got reworked: song count and total length up top, search in the top bar, a sort button (playlist order, recently added, title, artist, album, length), covers on every song, and play / shuffle are floating buttons now. dragging to reorder only works in playlist order with no search
- listen together is a proper button at the bottom of home now, with a headphones icon, instead of the tiny icon in the top bar
- people moved to the listen together screen (top right). it's gone from home and from your own profile
- edit profile is a full page now: bigger picture with a camera badge, you can change your name there, and favourite song / artist are picked from a search over the whole library. save only lights up when you changed something, and it asks before throwing edits away
- profiles show the same listening card as home, so gear has the radar on everyone's profile
- switching week / month / year on a profile no longer flashes the refresh spinner
- the top bar on a profile just says "profile" now, since the name is right under it
- fixed the lane intro where your name box was touching the diamond
- fixed a wrong tick in the add to playlist sheet. if you closed it while a song was still being added and quickly opened it for another song, that other song could show as already in the playlist when it wasn't
