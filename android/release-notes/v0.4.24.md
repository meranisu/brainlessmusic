## what changed

- stats: a new listening stats page. top songs, artists and albums, plus how much you've listened
- smart mixes: "Start radio" on any song, up to 6 daily mixes, and "Keep playing" keeps the queue going when it runs out (on by default)
- friends: see what your friends are playing right now, and a feed of their plays, likes and public playlists. one "Hide my activity" switch if you'd rather not show up
- on this day: a row on You with what you played on today's date in past years
- library lists open at once from the saved copy, then refresh with an "Updating…" note
- album art has its own cache with a size limit and a clear button in Settings
- the app checks for updates every hour and can notify you in the background. the update sheet waits until intros are done
- when nothing is fetching ahead, the app asks the server to get the next song ready early
- fixed: a restarted download service could lose its login

## upgrading

the update sheet will offer it. the server needs the latest update too (it deploys on its own)
