## what changed

- the songs tile in downloads is a proper collage now. up to six album covers, and the layout fits however many you have: one fills it, two side by side, three is one big and two small, all the way up to a 3+3
- one cover per album, so ten songs off the same album don't turn into ten copies of the same picture
- home got reshuffled: your card is right under shuffle now, then liked / playlists / hear less, then the week / month / year picker, then recently played and your top songs

## upgrading

install over v0.3.23, nothing else needed. songs you downloaded one at a time before v0.3.22 have no saved cover, so they won't show up in the collage until you download them again
