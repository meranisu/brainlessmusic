## what changed

the queue got a proper redo:

- swipe a song left to take it out of the queue (red), swipe right to hear it less often (blue). both have an undo
  at the bottom for a few seconds
- "less often" means shuffle skips it most of the time, not never. changed your mind? settings → playback →
  songs you hear less → reset. guests only get swipe left
- drag the handle on the right of a song to move it up or down
- search box at the top to find something in a long queue
- pulling the queue all the way up doesn't put the top under the camera hole anymore
- title's centred and the sides line up with the player now
