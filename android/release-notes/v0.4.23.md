## what changed

- fetch ahead: while you have signal, the app now saves the song that's playing and the next 3 in your queue to the phone, so they keep playing when you ride into a dead zone. they show up under Downloads, Cached
- Settings, Downloads has a new "Fetch ahead" row: Off, 1, 3, 5 or 10 songs. it uses mobile data too, and it fills the cache of played songs, so a small cache size means older songs get dropped sooner
- with data saver on, it saves the smaller copy
- it doesn't run inside a Listen together room
