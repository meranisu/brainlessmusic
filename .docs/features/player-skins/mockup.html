Player skins

Twelve looks for the Now Playing screen, each laid out like the controls of one rhythm game and painted in its colours. Pick the ones to build first. These are mockups: the song, the stats and the covers are examples, and the real covers come from your library.

The strip under each song title is the "personal" idea from Q115: your plays of that song plus a clear lamp worked out from how often you finish it versus skip it, in each game's own words.
